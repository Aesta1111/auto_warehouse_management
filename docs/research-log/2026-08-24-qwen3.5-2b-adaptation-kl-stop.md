## Objective

Run the active directive-demo frozen-LLM PPO adaptation from the existing
100,000-step parent through its validation/evaluation gates, while measuring
wall time, GPU utilization, and VRAM. The run should stop without changes if a
major error prevents progress.

## Hypothesis

Not applicable as a result claim. This was an exploratory prototype run; the
adapted PPO policy was expected to remain within the declared reference-KL
guard and produce a usable child manifest.

## Method

The existing parent checkpoint and failed-but-digest-matching preflight were
used exactly as configured. Qwen remained frozen and served by the pinned
llama.cpp runtime with 8,192 context, 99 GPU layers, deterministic decoding,
and reasoning disabled. The config's explicit exploratory
`require_passing_preflight: false` allowed adaptation to expose the known
preflight coverage failure; runtime validation and fallback accounting were
not bypassed. GPU samples were taken about every three minutes. After the
adaptation raised its KL-guard error, the adaptation process and llama.cpp
server were stopped and no later phase was started.

## Observations

- The pinned Qwen server loaded and served the adaptation without an OOM.
- The adaptation step log progressed continuously and reached the requested
  4,096-step boundary.
- The preflight artifact remained `passed: false`: it had eight valid measured
  responses and a passing latency limit, but only covered
  `service_requested_shelf`, with zero sensitivity states and no three-class
  sensitivity evidence.
- The final KL guard was the blocking error, not a model-output formatting or
  effect-validation error.

## Interpretation

The frozen Qwen runtime was operational for this workload, and the recorded
action boundary produced valid runtime actions. The child PPO update nonetheless
exceeded the declared reference-KL limit at the end of adaptation, so this run
cannot provide a valid adapted checkpoint or any parent/child evaluation claim.
The failed preflight also remains an independent unresolved gate.

## Decision

The frozen-LLM PPO adaptation phase did **not** complete. It reached 4,096
environment steps but failed its KL guard before final artifact publication;
therefore it did not complete and did not move on to selection validation.
Selection validation, confirmation validation, matched demo, and final seed
evaluation were not run. The prototype result is inconclusive and does not
advance any research phase or support an improvement claim.

## Problems and follow-up

- Preserve the failed run and its logs; do not treat the progress checkpoint as
  a completed child artifact.
- Do not rerun or change `max_reference_kl`, learning rate, preflight gates, or
  any other configuration in this halted run. Any future attempt must be a new,
  explicitly reviewed run ID.
